3. **Lack of Consequence Severity Differentiation**: The description mentions "penalties" but does not distinguish between minor sanctions (e.g., grade deduction) and major sanctions (e.g., academic dismissal) for different types of plagiarism. This is a hypothesis needing more information, as the description only confirms the existence of penalties, not their granularity.
