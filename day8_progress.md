## LLM Assessment Analysis (50 files)

Analyzed all 50 LLM review files from 
Ibrahim et al. (2026) across 5 models × 10 runs.

### Key Finding: Inconsistency Pattern
- ChatGPT 5.4: HIGH consistency across runs
- Claude 4.6: HIGH consistency across runs
- DeepSeek R1 7B: LOW consistency — different 
  top gaps identified each run
- Gemini 3 Flash: MEDIUM-LOW consistency
- Qwen 3.5 4B: LOW-MEDIUM consistency

### Research Implication
The inconsistency in gap identification 
(especially Section D) across runs for 
DeepSeek and Gemini demonstrates the 
exact problem my System B proposes to solve.

Knowledge graph grounding with structured 
facts about Q-learning limitations and 
agent heterogeneity in education should 
reduce this inter-run inconsistency.
