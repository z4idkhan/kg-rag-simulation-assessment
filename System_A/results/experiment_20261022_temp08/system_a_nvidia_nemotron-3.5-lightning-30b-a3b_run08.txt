It does not address whether detection varies by assignment type, student history, or the sophistication of the AI-assisted work. This matters because varying detection risk shapes the cost-benefit analysis of plagiarism types; a static probability may oversimplify the strategic dynamics the authors claim to observe. This is a hypothesis needing more information.
