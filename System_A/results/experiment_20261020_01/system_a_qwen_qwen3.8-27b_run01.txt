3.  **Gap:** The nature of "local peer networks" is unspecified.

If the network is highly clustered, AI-assisted plagiarism might spread differently than in a random network, affecting the generalizability of the finding that it remains prevalent under intervention.
    **Status:** Hypothesis needing more information; the description mentions "local peer networks" but provides no details on connectivity or structure.
